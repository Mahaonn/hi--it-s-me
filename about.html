Hi! I'm Evgeniia Kovzikova

As a technically savvy person with an artistic passion, I would like to improve my web development skills in HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript and React. Having completed several courses and hands-on training, I am ready to apply my knowledge to real-world projects.

I am open to various forms of collaboration, including permanent and contract roles, and my main goal is to gain hands-on experience.

My technical orientation has shaped my interest in understanding algorithms, while my affinity for creativity has been equally compelling. These two interests were harmoniously combined during my work as a sound engineer at Ukrainian Radio.

After the events of February 2022, I moved to Slovakia and started looking for a job that would satisfy my desire to combine technology and art. I was already familiar with Python, but I was more attracted to web development.

After a year of study, I realised that education alone is not enough to advance my career; practical experience is paramount. I am ready to respond to any opportunity with professionalism and quality work.
